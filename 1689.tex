\chapter{Of the Holy Scriptures} %1688

\firstparagraph{} %1688
\oldleadin{THE}{The} Holy Scripture is the only sufficient, certain, and infallible \R{}Rule of all Saving Knowledge, Faith, and Obedience; Although the light \R{}of Nature, and the works of Creation and Providence do so far manifest the goodness, wisdom, and power of God, as to leave men inexcusable; yet are they not sufficient to give that knowledge of God and his Will, which is necessary unto Salvation. \R{}Therefore it pleased the Lord at sundry times, and in divers manners, to reveal himself, and to  ndeclare that his Will unto his Church; and afterward for the better preserving, and propagating of the Truth, and for the more sure Establishment, and Comfort of the Church against the corruption of the Flesh, and the malice of Satan, and of the World, to commit the same wholly unto \R{}writing; which maketh the Holy Scriptures to be most necessary, those former ways of God’s revealing his Will unto his People being now ceased.

\footnotebegin
\C{1}{2 Timothy 3:15-17; Isaiah 8:20; Luke 16:29,31; Ephesians 2:20}
\C{2}{Romans 1:19-21,etc.; 2:14-15; Psalms 19:1-3}
\C{3}{Hebrews 1:1}
\C{4}{Proverbs 22:19-21; Romans 15:4; 2 Peter 1:19-20}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{2} %1688
Under the name of Holy Scripture, or the Word of God written, are now contained all the Books of the Old and New Testament, which are these:

\subparagraph{Of the Old Testament}
Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy, Joshua, Judges, Ruth, 1 Samuel, 2 Samuel, 1 Kings, 2 Kings, 1 Chronicles, 2 Chronicles, Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther, Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, The Song of Songs, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Lamentations, Ezekiel, Daniel, Hosea, Joel, Amos, Obadiah, Jonah, Micah, Nahum, Habakkuk, Zephaniah, Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi.

\subparagraph{Of the New Testament}
Matthew, Mark, Luke, John, The Acts of the Apostles, Paul's Epistle to the Romans, 1 Corinthians, 2 Corinthians, Galatians, Ephesians, Philippians, Colossians, 1 Thessalonians, 2 Thessalonians, 1 Timothy, 2 Timothy, to Titus, to Philemon, the Epistle to the Hebrews, the Epistle of James, the first and second Epistles of Peter, the first, second and third Epistles of John, the Epistle of Jude, the Revelation.

All which are given by the \R{}inspiration of God, to be the Rule of Faith and Life.

\footnotebegin
\C{5}{2 Timothy 3:16}
\footnoteend
      
\paragraph{3} %1688
The Books commonly called Apocrypha, not being of \R{}Divine Inspiration, are no part of the Canon (or Rule) of the Scripture, and, therefore are of no authority to the Church of God, nor to be any otherwise approved or made use of, than other Human Writings. 

\footnotebegin
\C{6}{Luke 24:27,44; Romans 3:2}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{4} %1688
The Authority of the Holy Scripture, for which it ought to be believed, dependeth not upon the testimony of any Man or Church, but wholly upon \R{}God, (who is truth itself), the Author thereof; therefore it is to be received, because it is the Word of God.

\footnotebegin
\C{7}{2 Peter 1:19-21; 2 Timothy 3:16; 2 Thessalonians 2:13; 1 John 5:9}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{5} %1688
We may be moved and induced by the testimony of the Church of God, to an high and reverent esteem of the Holy Scriptures; and the heavenliness of the Matter, the efficacy of the Doctrine, and the majesty of the Style, the consent of all the Parts, the scope of the Whole, (which is to give all glory to God) the full discovery it makes of the only Way of Man’s Salvation, and many other incomparable Excellencies, and entire Perfections thereof, are Arguments whereby it doth abundantly evidence itself to be the Word of God; yet notwithstanding, our \R{}full persuasion, and assurance of the infallible Truth, and Divine Authority thereof, is from the inward work of the Holy Spirit, bearing witness by and with the Word in our Hearts.

\footnotebegin
\C{8}{John 16:13,14; 1 Corinthians 2:10-12; 1 John 2:20,27}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{6} %1688
The whole Counsel of God concerning all things \R{}necessary for his own Glory, Man’s Salvation, Faith and Life, is either expressly set down or necessarily contained in the Holy Scripture; unto which nothing at any time is to be added, whether by new revelation of the Spirit, or traditions of men. 
Nevertheless, we acknowledge the inward \R{}Illumination of the Spirit of God, to be necessary for the saving understanding of such things as are revealed in the Word, and that there are some circumstances concerning the Worship of God, and Government of the Church, common to Human Actions and Societies; which are to be \R{}ordered by the Light of Nature, and Christian Prudence, according to the general Rules of the Word, which are always to be observed.

\footnotebegin
\C{9}{2 Timothy 3:15-17; Galatians 1:8-9}
\C{10}{John 6:45; 1 Corinthians 2:9-12}
\C{11}{1 Corinthians 11:13,14; 14:26,40}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{7} %1688
All things in Scripture are not alike \R{}plain in themselves, nor alike clear unto all; yet those things which are necessary to be known, believed, and observed for Salvation, are so \R{}clearly propounded, and opened in some place of Scripture or other, that not only the learned, but the unlearned, in a due use of ordinary means, may attain to a sufficient understanding of them.

\footnotebegin
\C{12}{2 Peter 3:16}
\C{13}{Psalms 19:7; Psalms 119:130}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{8} %1688
The Old Testament in \R{}\textit{Hebrew}, (which was the Native Language of the People of God of old), and the New Testament in \textit{Greek}, (which at the time of the writing of it) was most generally known to the Nations, being immediately inspired by God, and by his singular Care and Providence kept pure in all Ages, are therefore \R{}authentical; so as in all Controversies of Religion, the Church is finally to appeal unto them. \R{}But because these original Tongues are not known to all the People of God, who have a right unto, and interest in the Scriptures, and are commanded in the fear of God to read \R{}and search them, therefore they are to be translated into the vulgar Language of every Nation, unto which they \R{}come, that the Word of God dwelling \R{}plentifully in all, they may worship him in an acceptable manner, and through patience and comfort of the Scriptures may have hope.

\footnotebegin
\C{14}{Romans 3:2}
\C{15}{Isaiah 8:20}
\C{16}{Acts 15:15}
\C{17}{John 5:39}
\C{18}{1 Corinthians 14:6,9,11,12,24,28}
\C{19}{Colossians 3:16}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{9} %1688
The Infallible Rule of interpretation of Scripture is the \R{}Scripture itself: And therefore when there is a question about the true and full sense of any Scripture, (which is not manifold but one) it must be searched by other places, that speak more clearly.

\footnotebegin
\C{20}{2 Peter 1:20,21; Acts 15:15,16}
\footnoteend %1688

\paragraph{10} %1688
The Supreme Judge, by which all Controversies of Religion are to be determined, and all Decrees of Councils, Opinions of ancient Writers, Doctrines of Men, and private Spirits, are to be examined, and in whose sentence we are to rest, can be no other but the Holy Scripture delivered by the Spirit, into which \R{}Scripture so delivered, our Faith is finally resolved.

\footnotebegin
\ERRATA{
1689 has Matt 22:29,31
1719 has Matt 22:29,32
lbcw.pdf has Matt 22:29,31,32
31 seems the most to the point - Understand the Scriptues!
}
\C{21}{Matthew 22:29,31; Ephesians 2:20; Acts 28:23}
\footnoteend

\chapter{Of God and Of the Holy Trinity} %1688

\firstparagraph{} %1688
\oldleadin{THE}{The} Lord our God is but \R{}one \oldgrammar{onely}{only} living, and true God; whose \R{}subsistence is in and of himself, \R{}infinite in being and perfection, whose Essence cannot be comprehended by any but himself; \R{}a most pure Spirit, \R{}invisible, without body, parts, or passions, who only hath immortality, dwelling in the light, which no man can approach unto, who is \R{}immutable, \R{}immense, \R{}eternal, incomprehensible, \R{}Almighty, every way infinite, \R{}most holy, most wise, most free, most absolute, \R{}working all things according to the counsel of his own immutable, and most righteous will, \R{}for his own glory, most loving, gracious, merciful, longsuffering, abundant in goodness and truth, forgiving iniquity, transgression and sin, \R{}the rewarder of them that diligently seek him, and withal most just, \R{}and terrible in his judgments, \R{}hating all sin, and who will by no means clear the \R{}guilty.

\footnotebegin
\C{1}{1 Corinthians 8:4,6; Deuteronomy 6:4}
\C{2}{Jeremiah 10:10; Isaiah 48:12} 
\C{3}{Exodus 3:14}
\C{4}{John 4:24}
\C{5}{1 Timothy 1:17; Deuteronomy 4:15-16}
\C{6}{Malachi 3:6}
\C{7}{1 Kings 8:27; Jeremiah 23:23}
\C{8}{Psalms 90:2}
\C{9}{Genesis 17:1}
\C{10}{Isaiah 6:3}
\C{11}{Psalms 115:3; Isaiah 46:10}
\C{12}{Proverbs 16:4; Romans 11:36}
\C{13}{Exodus 34:6-7; Hebrews 11:6}
\C{14}{Nehemiah 9:32-33}
\C{15}{Psalms 5:5-6}
\C{16}{Exodus 34:7; Nahum 1:2-3}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{2} %1688
God, having all \R{}life, \R{}glory, \R{}goodness, blessedness, in and of himself, is alone in and unto himself all-sufficient, not \R{}standing in need of any Creature which he hath made, nor deriving any glory from them, but only manifesting his own glory in, by, unto, and upon them, he is the alone Fountain of all Being, \R{}of whom, through whom, and to whom are all things, and he hath most sovereign \R{}dominion over all creatures, to do by them, for them, or upon them, whatsoever himself pleaseth; in his sight \R{}all things are open and manifest, his knowledge is \R{}infinite, infallible, and independent upon the Creature, so as nothing is to him contingent or uncertain; he is most holy in all his Counsels, in \R{}all his Works, and in all his Commands; to him is due \R{}from Angels and Men, whatsoever worship, service, or obedience, as Creatures they owe unto the Creator, and whatever he is further pleased to require of them.

\footnotebegin
\C{17}{John 5:26}
\C{18}{Psalms 148:13} 
\C{19}{Psalms 119:68} 
\C{20}{Job 22:2-3}
\C{21}{Romans 11:34-36}
\C{22}{Daniel 4:25,34-35}
\C{23}{Hebrews 4:13}
\C{24}{Ezekiel 11:5; Acts 15:18}
\C{25}{Psalms 145:17}
\C{26}{Revelation 5:12-14}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{3} %1688
In this Divine and Infinite Being there are three subsistences, \R{}the Father, the Word, (or Son), and Holy Spirit, of one substance, power, and eternity, each having the whole Divine Essence, \R{}yet the Essence undivided, the Father is of none neither begotten nor proceeding, the Son is \R{}eternally begotten of the Father, the Holy Spirit \R{}proceeding from the Father and the Son, all infinite, without beginning, therefore but one God, who is not to be divided in Nature and Being, but distinguished by several peculiar, relative Properties, and personal Relations; which Doctrine of the Trinity is the Foundation of all our Communion with God, and comfortable dependence on him.

\footnotebegin 
\C{27}{1 John 5:7; Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14}
\C{28}{Exodus 3:14; John 14:11; 1 Corinthians 8:6}
\C{29}{John 1:14,18}
\C{30}{John 15:26; Galatians 4:6}
\footnoteend

\chapter{Of God’s Decree} %1688

\firstparagraph{} %1688
\oldleadin{GOD}{God} hath \R{}\textit{decreed} in himself, from all eternity, by the most wise and holy Counsel of his own Will, freely and unchangeably, all things whatsoever comes to pass; yet so as thereby is God neither the Author of sin, \R{}nor hath fellowship with any therein, nor is violence offered to the will of the Creature, nor yet is the liberty, or contingency of second Causes taken away, but rather \R{}established, in which appears his Wisdom in disposing all things, and Power and Faithfulness \R{}in accomplishing his \textit{Decree}.

\footnotebegin
\C{1}{Isaiah 46:10; Ephesians 1:11; Hebrews 6:17; Romans 9:15,18}
\C{2}{James 1:13,17; 1 John 1:5}
\C{3}{Acts 4:27,28; John 19:11}
\C{4}{Numbers 23:19; Ephesians 1:3-5}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{2} %1688
Although God knoweth whatsoever may, or can come to pass upon all \R{}supposed conditions; yet hath he not \textit{decreed} anything, \R{}because he foresaw it as future, or as that which would come to pass upon such conditions.

\footnotebegin
\C{5}{Acts 15:18}
\C{6}{Romans 9:11,13,16,18}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{3} %1688
By the \textit{Decree} of God, for the manifestation of his glory, \R{}some Men and Angels are predestinated, or foreordained to Eternal Life through Jesus Christ, to the \R{}praise of his glorious grace; others being left to act in their sin to their \R{}just condemnation, to the praise of his glorious justice.

\footnotebegin

\C{7}{1 Timothy 5:21; Matthew 25:41}
\C{8}{Ephesians 1:5,6}
\C{9}{Romans 9:22,23; Jude 4}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{4} %1688
These Angels and Men thus predestinated, and foreordained, are particularly, and unchangeably designed; and their \R{}number so certain and definite, that it cannot be either increased, or diminished.

\footnotebegin
\C{10}{2 Timothy 2:19; John 13:18}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{5} %1688
Those of Mankind \R{}that are predestinated to life, God before the foundation of the world was laid, according to his eternal and immutable Purpose, and the secret counsel and good pleasure of his Will, hath chosen in Christ unto everlasting glory, out of his mere free grace and love; \R{}without any other thing in the creature as a condition or cause moving him thereunto.

\footnotebegin
\C{11}{Ephesians 1:4,9,11; Romans 8:30; 2 Timothy 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:9}

\C{12}{Romans 9:13,16; Ephesians 1:6,12}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{6} %1688
As God hath appointed the Elect unto glory, so he hath, by the eternal and most free purpose of his Will, foreordained \R{}all the means thereunto, wherefore they who are elected, being \oldgrammar{fal'n}{fallen} in \textit{Adam}, \R{}are redeemed by Christ, are effectually \R{}called unto faith in Christ, by his Spirit working in due season, are justified, adopted, sanctified, and kept by his power through faith \R{}unto salvation; neither are any other redeemed by Christ, or effectually called, justified, adopted, sanctified, and saved, but the Elect \R{}only.

\footnotebegin
\C{13}{1 Peter 1:2; 2 Thessalonians 2:13}
\C{14}{1 Thessalonians 5:9,10}
\C{15}{Romans 8:30; 2 Thessalonians 2:13}
\C{16}{1 Peter 1:5}
\C{17}{John 10:26, 17:9, 6:64}

\paragraph{7} %1688
The Doctrine of this high mystery of predestination is to be handled with special prudence and care, that men attending the will of God revealed in his Word, and yielding obedience thereunto, may, from the certainty of their effectual vocation, be assured of their \R{}eternal election; so shall this doctrine afford matter \R{}of praise, reverence, and admiration of God, and \R{}of humility, diligence, and abundant \R{}consolation to all that sincerely obey the gospel.

\footnotebegin
\C{18}{1 Thessalonians 1:4-5; 2 Peter 1:10}
\C{19}{Ephesians 1:6; Romans 11:33}

\footnoteend

\chapter{Of Creation} %1688

\firstparagraph{} %1688
\oldleadin{IN}{In} the beginning it pleased God the Father, \R{}Son, and Holy Spirit, for the manifestation of the glory of \R{}his Eternal Power, Wisdom, and Goodness, to \textit{create} or \textit{make} the World, and all things therein, \R{}whether visible or invisible, in the space of six days, and all very good.

\footnotebegin
\C{1}{John 1:2,3; Hebrews 1:2; Job 26:13}

After God had made all other Creatures, he \textit{created} \R{}Man, Male and Female, with \R{}reasonable and immortal Souls, rendering them fit unto that Life to God, for which they were \textit{created}; being \R{}made after the image of God, in knowledge, righteousness, and true holiness; having the Law of God \R{}written in their hearts, and power to fulfill it; and yet under a possibility of transgressing, being left to the liberty of their own will, which was \R{}subject to change.

\footnotebegin

Besides the Law written in their Hearts, they received \R{}a Command not to eat of the Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil; which whil'st they kept, they were happy in their Communion with God, and had Dominion \R{}over the Creatures.

\footnotebegin
\C{9}{Genesis 2:17}
\C{10}{Genesis 1:26,28}
\footnoteend

\chapter{Of Divine Providence} %1688

\firstparagraph{} %1688
\oldleadin{GOD}{God} the good \textit{Creator} of all things, in \textit{his} infinite power, and wisdom, doth \R{}uphold, direct, dispose, and govern all Creatures, and Things, from the greatest even to the \R{}least, by \textit{his} most wise and holy Providence, to the end for the which they were \textit{created}, according unto \textit{his} infallible foreknowledge, and the free and immutable counsel of \textit{his} \R{}own Will; to the praise of the glory of \textit{his} wisdom, power, justice, infinite goodness and mercy.

\footnotebegin
\C{1}{Hebrews 1:3; Job 38:11; Isaiah 46:10,11; Psalms 135:6}
\C{2}{Matthew 10:29-31}
\C{3}{Ephesians 1:11}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{2} %1688
Although in relation to the foreknowledge and \textit{Decree of God}, the first cause, all things come to pass \R{}immutably and infallibly; so that there is not anything, befalls any \R{}by chance, or without \textit{his Providence}; yet by the same \textit{Providence} he ordereth them to fall out, according to the nature of second causes, either \R{}necessarily, freely, or contingently.

\footnotebegin
\C{4}{Acts 2:23}
\C{5}{Proverbs 16:33}
\C{6}{Genesis 8:22}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{3} %1688
God, in \textit{his} ordinary \textit{Providence} \R{}maketh use of means; yet is free \R{}to work, without, \R{}above, and \R{}against them at \textit{his} pleasure.

\footnotebegin
\C{7}{Acts 27:31,44; Isaiah 55:10-11}
\C{8}{Hosea 1:7}
\C{9}{Romans 4:19-21}
\C{10}{Daniel 3:27}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{4} %1688
The Almighty Power, unsearchable Wisdom, and infinite Goodness of \textit{God}, so far manifest themselves in \textit{his Providence}, that \textit{his} determinate Counsel \R{}extendeth itself even to the first Fall, and all other sinful Actions both of Angels and Men; (and that not by a bare permission) which also he most wisely and powerfully \R{}boundeth, and otherwise ordereth and governeth, in a manifold dispensation to \textit{his} most holy \R{}ends; yet so, as the sinfulness of their acts proceedeth only from the Creatures, and not from \textit{God}, who being most holy and righteous, neither is nor can be the Author or \R{}Approver of sin.

\footnotebegin
\C{11}{Romans 11:32-34; 2 Samuel 24:1; 1 Chronicles 21:1}
\C{12}{2 Kings 19:28; Psalms 76:10}
\C{13}{Genesis 50:20; Isaiah 10:6,7,12}
\C{14}{Psalms 50:21; 1 John 2:16}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{5} %1688
The most wise, righteous, and gracious \textit{God} doth oftentimes leave for a season \textit{his} own children to manifold temptations, and the corruptions of their own heart, to chastise them for their former sins, or to discover unto them the hidden strength of corruption, and deceitfulness of their hearts, \R{}that they may be humbled; and to raise them to a more close and constant dependence for their support upon himself; and to make them more watchful against all future occasions of sin, and for other just and holy ends. 
So that whatsoever befalls any of his Elect is by his appointment, for his glory, \R{}and their good.

\footnotebegin

}

These holy appointments are to be administered by those only, who are qualified and thereunto called according \R{}to the Commission of Christ.

\oldleadin{BAptism}{Baptism} is an Ordinance of the New Testament, ordained by Jesus Christ, to be unto the Party baptized, a Sign of his fellowship with him, in his Death, \R{}and Resurrection; of his being Engrafted into him; of \R{}Remission of Sins; and of his giving \R{}giving up unto God, through Jesus Christ, to live and walk in newness of Life.

\C{1}{Romans 6:3-5; Colossians 2:12; Galatians 3:27}

\chapter{Of the Lord’s Supper}

\firstparagraph{} %1688
\oldleadin{THE}{The} Supper of the Lord Jesus, was instituted by him, the same Night wherein he was betrayed, to be observed in his Churches unto the end of the World, for the perpetual remembrance, and shewing forth the Sacrifice of himself in his Death, \R{}confirmation of the Faith of Believers in all the benefits thereof, their spiritual nourishment, and growth in him, their further \oldgrammar{ingagement}{engagement} in, and to, all Duties which they owe unto him; \R{}and to be a Bond and Pledge of their Communion with him, and with each other.

\footnotebegin
\C{1}{1 Corinthians 11:23-26}
\C{2}{1 Corinthians 10:16-17,21}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{2} %1688
In this Ordinance Christ is not offered up to his Father, nor any real Sacrifice made at all for Remission of Sin of the Quick or Dead, but only a Memorial of that \R{}one offering up of himself, by himself, upon the Cross, once for all; and a Spiritual Oblation of all \R{}possible Praise unto God for the same. So that the \textit{Popish} Sacrifice of the Mass (as they call it) is most abominable, injurious to Christ’s own Sacrifice, the alone Propitiation for all the Sins of the Elect.

\footnotebegin
\C{3}{Hebrews 9:25-26,28}
\C{4}{1 Corinthians 11:24; Matthew 26:26-27}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{3} %1688
The Lord Jesus hath in this Ordinance, appointed his Ministers to Pray, and Bless the Elements of Bread and Wine, and thereby to set them apart from a common to a holy use, and to take and break the Bread; to take the Cup, \R{}and (they communicating also themselves) to give both to the Communicants. 

\footnotebegin
\C{5}{1 Corinthians 11:23-26, etc.}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{4} %1688
The \oldgrammar{denyal}{denial} of the Cup to the People, worshipping the Elements, the lifting them up, or carrying them about for adoration, and reserving them for any pretended religious use, \R{}are all contrary to the Nature of this Ordinance, and to the Institution of Christ.

\footnotebegin
\C{6}{Matthew 26:26-28; 15:9; Exodus 20:4-5}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{5} %1688
The outward Elements in this Ordinance, duly set apart to the use ordained by Christ, have such relation to him crucified, as that truly, although in terms used figuratively, they are sometimes called by the names of the things they represent, to wit, the \R{}Body and Blood of Christ, albeit in substance, and nature, they still remain truly, and only \R{}Bread, and Wine, as they were before.

\footnotebegin
\C{7}{1 Corinthians 11:27}
\C{8}{1 Corinthians 11:26,28}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{6} %1688
That Doctrine which maintains a change of the substance of Bread and Wine, into the substance of Christ’s Body and Blood (commonly called Transubstantiation) by consecration of a Priest, or by any other way, is repugnant not to Scripture \R{}alone, but even to common sense and reason, overthroweth the \R{}nature of the Ordinance, and hath been and is the cause of manifold Superstitions, yea, of gross Idolatries.

\footnotebegin
\C{9}{Acts 3:21; Luke 24:6,39}
\C{10}{1 Corinthians 11:24-25}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{7} %1688
Worthy Receivers, outwardly partaking of the visible Elements in this Ordinance, do then also inwardly by Faith, really and indeed, yet not carnally and corporally, but spiritually receive, and feed upon Christ crucified, \R{}and all the benefits of his Death; the Body and Blood of Christ being then not Corporally, or Carnally, but spiritually present to the Faith of Believers, in that Ordinance, as the Elements themselves are to their outward senses.

\footnotebegin
\C{11}{1 Corinthians 10:16; 11:23-26}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{8} %1688
All ignorant and ungodly Persons, as they are unfit to enjoy Communion \R{}with Christ, so are they unworthy of the Lord’s Table, and cannot, without great sin against him, while they remain such, partake of these Holy Mysteries, \R{}or be admitted thereunto: yea, whosoever shall receive unworthily, are guilty of the Body and Blood of the Lord, eating and drinking Judgment to themselves.

\footnotebegin
\C{12}{2 Corinthians 6:14-15}
\C{13}{1 Corinthians 11:29; Matthew 7:6}
\footnoteend

\chapter{Of the State of Man after Death and Of the Resurrection of the Dead}

\firstparagraph{} %1688
\oldleadin{THE}{The} Bodies of Men after Death return to dust, \R{}and see corruption; but their Souls, which neither die nor sleep, having an immortal subsistence, immediately \R{}return to God who gave them: the Souls of the Righteous being then made perfect in holiness, are received into Paradise, where they are with \textit{Christ}, and behold the Face of \textit{God}, in light \R{}and glory, waiting for the full Redemption of their Bodies; and the Souls of the Wicked are cast into Hell; where they remain in torment and utter darkness, reserved to \R{}the judgment of the Great Day; besides these two places, for Souls separated from their Bodies, the Scripture acknowledgeth none.

\footnotebegin
\C{1}{Genesis 3:19; Acts 13:36}
\C{2}{Ecclesiastes 12:7}
\C{3}{Luke 23:43; 2 Corinthians 5:1,6,8; Philemon 1:23; Hebrews 12:3}
\C{4}{Jude 6-7; 1 Peter 3:19; Luke 16:23-24}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{2} %1688
At the Last Day, such of the Saints as are found alive, shall not sleep, but be \R{}changed; and all the dead shall be raised up with the selfsame Bodies, and \R{}none other; although with different \R{}Qualities, which shall be united again to their Souls for ever.

\footnotebegin
\C{5}{1 Corinthians 15:51-52; 1 Thessalonians 4:17}
\C{6}{Job 19:26-27}
\C{7}{1 Corinthians 15:42-43}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{3} %1688
The Bodies of the unjust shall, by the power of Christ, be raised to dishonour; the Bodies of the just, by his spirit, unto honour, \R{}and be made conformable to his own glorious Body.

\footnotebegin
\C{8}{Acts 24:15; John 5:28-29; Philemon 3:21}
\footnoteend

\chapter{Of the Last Judgment}

\firstparagraph{} %1688
\oldleadin{GOD}{God} hath appointed a Day wherein he will judge the World in Righteousness, by \R{}Jesus Christ; to whom all power and judgment is given of the Father; in which Day not only the \R{}Apostate Angels shall be judged, but likewise all Persons that have lived upon the Earth, shall appear before the Tribunal of Christ, \R{}to give an account of their Thoughts, Words, and Deeds, and to receive according to what they have done in the Body, whether good or evil.

\footnotebegin
\C{1}{Acts 17:31; John 5:22,27}
\C{2}{1 Corinthians 6:3; Jude 6}
\C{3}{2 Corinthians 5:10; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 12:36; Romans 14:10,12; Matthew 25:32, etc.}
\footnoteend

\paragraph{2} %1688
The end of God’s appointing this Day, is for the manifestation of the Glory of his Mercy, in the Eternal Salvation of the Elect; \R{}and of his Justice, in the Eternal Damnation of the Reprobate, who are wicked and disobedient; for then shall the Righteous go into Everlasting Life, and receive that fulness of Joy, and Glory with Everlasting Reward, in the presence \R{}of the Lord: but the Wicked, who know not God, and obey not the Gospel of Jesus Christ, shall be cast into Eternal Torments, and \R{}punished with Everlasting Destruction, from the presence of the Lord, and from the glory of his Power.

\footnotebegin
\C{4}{Romans 9:22-23}
\C{5}{Matthew 25:21,34; 2 Timothy 4:8}
\C{6}{Matthew 25:46; Mark 9:48; 2 Thessalonians 1:7-10}
\footnoteend
 
\paragraph{3} 
As Christ would have us to be certainly persuaded, that there shall be a day of judgment, both \R{}to deter all men from sin, and for the greater \R{}consolation of the godly in their adversity, so will he have the Day unknown to men, that they may shake off all carnal security, and be always watchful, because they know not at what Hour the \R{}Lord will come, and may ever be prepared to say, \R{}\textit{Come, Lord Jesus, come quickly. Amen.}

\C{7}{2 Corinthians 5:10-11}
\C{8}{2 Thessalonians 1:5-7}

\footnoteend
